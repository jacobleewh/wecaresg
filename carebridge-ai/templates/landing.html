How It Works

From plain-language message to matched support, in three steps.

01

Describe your situation

Type it in plain language — Singlish welcome — over web chat or Telegram. No forms, no jargon.

02

Understand your needs

Your details are organised into a clear summary, with sensitive identifiers masked before a case is saved.

03

Matched & routed

Real SG schemes are ranked by eligibility and streamed live to an available case worker for review.

Instant Triage

Describe your situation in plain language and get matched in seconds — no waiting room, no queue number.

Web + Telegram

Submit from a browser or our Telegram bot — every case syncs live to the case worker dashboard.

Case Worker Collaboration

Accept, decline, or refer a case to a colleague by their Worker ID — only one worker ever owns a case.

Clear Case Summaries

Understand possible options and why they may be relevant before deciding whether to ask a case worker for help.

PDPA-Aware Masking

NRIC and phone numbers are automatically masked before any data is stored or analyzed.

Live Case Feed

Vulnerability scores, matched schemes, and gaps stream to case workers in real time via SSE.
